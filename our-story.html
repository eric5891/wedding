Our Story

Eric and Polina were both born in Moscow, raised in Russian-speaking families, and carry that shared heritage with them. Though they now speak English most of the time, there's something irreplaceable about slipping back into Russian—a quiet joke, a whispered thought, a language that still feels like home.

Eric moved to Brooklyn at the age of three and later settled in Manhattan in his twenties. Years later, Polina arrived in Manhattan at 22 to pursue her master's degree and a career in marketing.

They almost met there—but timing had other plans. Before Polina moved to Miami, she took a photo on a Soho doorstep—Eric's former home—just a month after he had left the city. They had missed each other by weeks.

Then everything aligned.

Less than a month after Polina arrived in Miami, they met—and their first date at Ariete felt effortless and unforgettable.

From that moment on, they became inseparable. What they shared was both an emotional connection and a deep sense of clarity: they valued the same things—ambition, family, and kindness—and being together felt like a blessing from the very beginning.

On December 27, 2024, Eric proposed at the Pérez Art Museum Miami. Polina said yes.

Now, they begin a new chapter as husband and wife.
