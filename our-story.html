Our Story

Eric and Polina's story begins long before they ever met. Both were born in Moscow and raised in Russian-speaking families, carrying a shared heritage that still shapes their world. Though life has brought them into English, there is something intimate and irreplaceable about returning to Russian—language that still feels like home.

Eric moved to Brooklyn at the age of three and later built his life in Manhattan, where he founded Khrom Capital. Years later, Polina arrived in the same city at twenty-two, pursuing her master's degree and beginning her career in marketing. They came remarkably close to meeting—but timing had other plans. Just weeks after Eric left New York for Miami, Polina unknowingly stood on the doorstep of his former home and even took a photo there. They had missed each other by days.

Then everything aligned.

Less than a month after Polina arrived in Miami, they met—and their first date at Ariete felt effortless and unforgettable.

From that moment on, they became inseparable. What they shared was both an emotional connection and a deep sense of clarity: they valued the same things—ambition, family, and kindness—and being together felt like a blessing from the very beginning.

On December 27, 2024, Eric proposed at the Pérez Art Museum Miami. Polina said yes.

Now, they begin a new chapter as husband and wife.
