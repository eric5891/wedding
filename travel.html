Getting There

The nearest international airport is Naples International Airport (NAP), approximately 65 km from Ravello. Rome's Fiumicino Airport (FCO) is another option, with high-speed train service to Naples.

From Naples, you can reach Ravello by private car transfer (approx. 75 minutes) along the stunning Amalfi coastal road.
