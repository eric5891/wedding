Travel

Ravello sits high in the hills above the Amalfi Coast.
Here is everything you need to plan your journey to this magical corner of southern Italy.

Getting There

The nearest international airport is Naples International Airport (NAP), approximately 65 km from Ravello. Rome's Fiumicino Airport (FCO) is another option, with high-speed train service to Naples.

Naples to Ravello

From Naples, you can reach Ravello by private car transfer (approx. 75 minutes) along the stunning Amalfi coastal road, or by ferry from Naples to Amalfi followed by a short drive up the hillside.

Where to Stay

Ravello offers charming hotels with spectacular views. We recommend booking in Ravello, Amalfi, or Positano. A room block will be available — details to follow.

While You're There

Explore the Amalfi Coast — visit Positano's pastel cliffside villages, taste fresh limoncello in Amalfi, take a boat to the island of Capri, or wander the ancient ruins of Pompeii. Make it a holiday!
