The Venue

Villa Cimbrone

Ravello • Amalfi Coast • Italy

Perched high above the Amalfi Coast, Villa Cimbrone is a historic estate renowned for its breathtaking Terrace of Infinity — where ancient marble busts frame panoramic views of the Mediterranean Sea stretching to eternity. Among centuries-old gardens, flowering pergolas, and sun-drenched stone, we will celebrate our love in one of the most beautiful places on earth.

Getting There

Ravello sits high in the hills above the Amalfi Coast. Here is everything you need to plan your journey to this magical corner of southern Italy.

Flying In

The nearest international airport is Naples International Airport (NAP), approximately 65 km from Ravello. Rome's Fiumicino Airport (FCO) is another option, with high-speed train service to Naples.

Getting to Ravello

From Naples, you can reach Ravello by private car transfer (approx. 75 minutes) along the stunning coastal road, or by ferry from Naples to Amalfi followed by a short drive up the hillside.

Where to Stay

Ravello offers charming hotels with spectacular views. We recommend booking in Ravello, Amalfi, or Positano. A room block will be available — details to follow.

While You're There

Explore the Amalfi Coast — visit Positano's pastel cliffside, taste limoncello in Amalfi, take a boat to Capri, or wander the ancient ruins of Pompeii. Make it a holiday!
