Our Story

Some love stories begin with a grand gesture.
Ours, fittingly, began with a spilled glass of wine.

We first crossed paths at a friend's dinner party in June 2020. Eric spilled wine on Polina's dress, and somehow that clumsy moment turned into the most beautiful beginning. We talked for hours that evening, discovering shared passions for travel, good food, and the kind of laughter that makes your sides ache.

We spent our early days exploring the city together, finding our favorite coffee shops and hidden restaurants, building a world that felt entirely our own.

Our first big adventure together took us to the mountains of Colorado in December 2021. Between snowball fights and cozy evenings by the fire, we realized we never wanted to stop exploring the world — or life — together.

After that, every trip brought us closer: weekends on the coast, summers abroad, quiet mornings that stretched into afternoons. We learned each other's rhythms and found that the best moments were always the simplest ones — together.

And then, on a quiet August evening in 2025, walking along the beach at sunset, Eric got down on one knee. Through happy tears, Polina said "yes" before he even finished asking.

Some might call it fate, others a beautiful coincidence.
But maybe it was always going to be Polina and Eric —
love just had to catch up with them.

Travel

Getting There

The nearest international airport is Naples International Airport (NAP), approximately 65 km from Ravello. Rome's Fiumicino Airport (FCO) is another option, with high-speed train service to Naples.

Naples to Ravello

From Naples, you can reach Ravello by private car transfer (approx. 75 minutes) along the stunning Amalfi coastal road, or by ferry from Naples to Amalfi followed by a short drive up the hillside to Ravello.

Where to Stay

Ravello offers charming hotels with spectacular views of the Amalfi Coast. We recommend booking in Ravello, Amalfi, or Positano. A room block will be available — details to follow.

While You're There

Explore the Amalfi Coast — visit Positano's pastel cliffside villages, taste fresh limoncello in Amalfi, take a boat to the island of Capri, or wander the ancient ruins of Pompeii. Make it a holiday!

FAQs

What is the dress code?

Black tie optional. The ceremony and reception take place outdoors on the villa's garden terraces, so we recommend elegant footwear suitable for stone paths and grass.

Can I bring a plus one?

Due to venue capacity, we are only able to accommodate guests who are listed on the invitation. If you have any questions, please reach out to us directly.

Will transportation be provided?

Yes, shuttle service will be arranged between select hotels in Ravello and Villa Cimbrone. Details will be shared closer to the date.

What about the weather?

August in Ravello is warm and sunny, with temperatures around 28–32°C (82–90°F). Evenings are comfortably warm. Rain is rare but possible — we'll have a backup plan just in case.

Is there parking at the venue?

Villa Cimbrone is accessible only on foot through Ravello's charming pedestrian streets. We recommend arriving via shuttle or taxi to Ravello's main piazza, from which it is a short walk through the gardens to the villa.
