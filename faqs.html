FAQs

What is the dress code?

Black tie optional. The ceremony and reception take place outdoors on the villa's garden terraces, so we recommend elegant footwear suitable for stone paths and grass.

Can I bring a plus one?

Due to venue capacity, we are only able to accommodate guests who are listed on the invitation. If you have any questions, please reach out to us directly.

Will transportation be provided?

Yes, shuttle service will be arranged between select hotels in Ravello and Villa Cimbrone. Details will be shared closer to the date.

What about the weather?

August in Ravello is warm and sunny, with temperatures around 28–32°C (82–90°F). Evenings are comfortably warm. Rain is rare but possible — we'll have a backup plan just in case.

Is there parking at the venue?

Villa Cimbrone is accessible only on foot through Ravello's charming pedestrian streets. We recommend arriving via shuttle or taxi to Ravello's main piazza, from which it is a short walk through the gardens to the villa.
