Dress Code

Each event of our wedding weekend carries its own mood and atmosphere. We've thoughtfully curated dress codes to reflect the spirit of each moment.

Thursday, August 6

Welcome Dinner

Cocktail Attire · Celestial Theme

An evening beneath the stars at Villa Cimbrone.

Ladies: Cocktail dresses in shades of blue, silver, gray, or lavender. Subtle celestial details are welcome.

Gentlemen: Suit or linen suit in classic tones.

Friday, August 7

Capri Day

Relaxed Coastal Chic

Set sail for the island of Capri for a day of boating, swimming and soaking in the Mediterranean sun.

Ladies & Gentlemen: Summer attire and swimwear.

Saturday, August 8

Wedding Celebration

Black Tie

Our main celebration — formal, vibrant and unforgettable.

Ladies: Floor-length gowns in shades of pink or red (orange is also welcome).

Gentlemen: Black tuxedo with black bow tie.
